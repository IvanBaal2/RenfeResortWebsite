Bright Room with Signature Style

Our resort accommodations combine a clean, minimalist aesthetic with premium comfort. All configurations showcase warm light-wood herringbone flooring, crisp white linens, and our signature mustard-yellow accent throws and pillows that add a cohesive splash of style to every room.
The Double Studio Layout (Left Photo): A bright, spacious optideal for couples or solo travelers. It features large sliding glass doors opening to a private balcony, wall-mounted flat-screen setups, and dedicated air conditioning for personalized climate control.
The Twin Suite Layouts (Middle & Right Photos): A masterfully optimized space for friends or colleagues featuring two single beds. These rooms are designed for ultimate rest, complete with textured headboards, framed artwork, energy-efficient recessed lighting, and high-quality blackout curtains.

Modern Suite with Distinct Living

Aesthetic with multi-functional spaces. Featuring polished, high-gloss stone flooring throughout, the suite uses warm wood paneling, structured architectural lighting, and refined textiles to create an inviting, high-end atmosphere.
The Entrance & Flow (Left Photo): A long, elegant hallway flanked by sleek, mirrored floor-to-ceiling sliding closets that maximize storage. The corridor opens up directly into a deeply recessed, private living and dining area.
The Bedroom Sanctuary (Middle Photo): A dedicated sleeping area anchored by a massive, floor-to-ceiling light wood paneled headboard. The bed is dressed in premium white linens and layered with a textured, contemporary grey-patterned blanket, framed perfectly by warm bedside lamps.
The Living & Lounge Space (Right Photo): A distinct lounge zone designed for relaxation or casual meetings. It features a secondary large bed setup framed against a striking geometric star-patterned accent wall, a low-profile wooden coffee table, a sleek accent chair, and heavy drapes for ultimate privacy.

Multi-Level Suite

This expansive, multi-story suite provides residential-style living with maximum privacy and space. Grounded by rich, dark wood flooring and clean white walls, the multi-level layout features architectural split-level stairs, integrated ceiling spotlights, and distinct living, dining, and sleeping zones.
The Living & Dining Area (Left Photo): A spacious open-concept main floor that accommodates travel luggage seamlessly. It features a contemporary round dining table with woven-backed chairs, a secondary bedding/lounge alcove, a workspace desk, and a low-hanging geometric pendant light fixture.
The Upper Landing & Staircase (Middle Photo): An architectural transition space featuring an open metal-railing staircase. This landing serves as a functional foyer equipped with a stylish black wire Acapulco accent chair and gives direct access to private bathrooms and bedrooms.
The Main Bedroom Sanctuary (Right Photo): A deeply private upstairs bedroom featuring two twin beds seamlessly pushed together. The space is anchored by a dark wood accent headboard wall with built-in ambient halo lighting, a wall-mounted flat-screen TV, and heavy privacy curtains for a restful night.

Amenities

EVENING SHOWS

Enjoy spectacular evening shows featuring live music, cultural performances, and entertainment for the whole family. Unwind under the stars as our talented performers light up the night with unforgettable shows, creating memories that will last long after your vacation ends.

FUN FOR KIDS

Let your little ones dive into a world of adventure! Our resort offers dedicated kids, shallow splash pools, and supervised daily activities. From arts and crafts to exciting outdoor games, we ensure non stop fun and safe entertainment for our youngest guests, giving parents a chance to relax.

POOL EXPERINCE

Dive into pure relaxation at our expansive outdoor pools. Whether you're swimming laps in the morning sun, lounging on comfortable sunbeds with a good book, or enjoying a refreshing cocktail by the water at sunset, our poolside oasis offers breathtaking views and ultimate tranquility.

BEAUTIFUL SANDY BEACH

Step directly onto our pristine, soft sandy beach. Perfect for sunbathing, building sandcastles with the kids, or simply strolling along the shoreline. With complimentary beach umbrellas, loungers, and a variety of beachside water activities, your perfect seaside escape awaits just steps from your room.

SPORTS & RECREATION

Stay active during your getaway with our wide range of sports and recreation facilities. Whether you want to challenge your family to a game of beach volleyball, play a few sets of tennis, or explore the scenic coastal trails on a guided nature walk, there are endless ways to keep your energy high and your spirits lifted.
